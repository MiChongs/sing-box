Health checks answer a coarse question: does this member work right now, and is it steady? They do not decide which node is better (that is the job of the [comprehensive score](../scoring/)), but members that fail or wobble are excluded during [filtering](../how-it-works/#filtering-members).

## Periodic checks

- **When**: first 10 s after start, then every `interval` (5 minutes by default), only while the group is **in use**: a new connection within `idle_timeout` (30 minutes by default). An idle phone spends no battery on them.
- **How**: an HTTP HEAD request to `url` (`https://www.gstatic.com/generate_204` by default) through every member, up to 15 s, at most 10 at once; `expected_status` decides which status codes pass. Members of type `block` or `dns` and the built-in `Compatible` are not checked.
- **Reuse**: groups share one prober. A member result younger than half an `interval` is reused instead of requested again.
- **Where results go**: into the delay history dashboards show; into the score as the "probe delay" (valid for 2 minutes after each check, see [Freshness](../scoring/#freshness)); and into the last-10 history of the stability check.

### Judging a member unavailable

- A working member is judged unavailable after **2 failed checks in a row**; one failure may just be the check URL. A member already unavailable stays so after one more failure.
- One success makes it available again.
- A member never checked counts as available.

Unavailable members are excluded at the first filtering level; if every member is unavailable, filtering loosens to ignore health, so a false negative never leaves a connection with nowhere to go.

### Group delay tests

Running a delay test on a Smart group in the dashboard (`GET /group/{name}/delay`) tests every member, and the results count toward health and the score as well. **Note: a group delay test releases the pinned node**, as in mihomo. Testing the group as a single proxy (`GET /proxies/{name}/delay`) does not release the pin; it measures through the current choice.

## Stability

A node that works but wobbles is worse than one that is plainly down: delays jump around, the odd request times out, and users notice the stutter. Smart reuses mihomo's **MAD (median absolute deviation)** check. Every 10 minutes (first 5 minutes after start) it looks at each member's last 10 health checks:

- **Anomaly**: the latest check failed, or the latest two delays are both above the threshold. Threshold = median + k × 1.4826 × MAD, with k = 3.5 below 10 samples and 2.5 otherwise (when the MAD is 0, mean + 2 standard deviations);
- **Unstable**: more than half of the last 3 checks failed, or 1.4826 × MAD ÷ median (the robust coefficient of variation) is 0.6 or more;
- **Worse**: thresholds are graded (up to 500 ms grade 1, up to 1000 ms grade 2, up to 2000 ms grade 3, beyond that grade 4), and the grade rose since the last check, or moved from grade 2 or better to grade 3 or worse.

Any of these blocks the node **group-wide for 12 minutes** (reason `unstable`): the first filtering level excludes it and the ranking shows `Alive: false`. With fewer than 3 valid samples, only failures count. No stability check runs during a failure flood.

## Node-level failures

Besides health checks, real connections tell Smart when a node is in trouble. A dial failure is a **node-level** failure: the node's failure streak grows on every site, and it enters a backoff starting at 5 minutes, doubling up to 6 hours. With a streak of 3, the node is not eligible as long as some other member is healthy.

Recovery comes from real traffic, not health checks: once 4 different connections in turn prove it receives data again, the failure state clears. Details in [Score and selection](../scoring/#failure-state). `FailStreak` and `BackedOff` in the ranking show both states.

## Manual group-wide blocks

If you know a node's line is bad tonight, take it out of the group for a while:

```bash
curl -X POST 'http://127.0.0.1:9090/smart/groups/auto/block/HK-01?duration=2h'
curl -X DELETE 'http://127.0.0.1:9090/smart/groups/auto/block/HK-01'   # lift early
```

Without `duration` it lasts 30 minutes. Like stability blocks, a group-wide block excludes the node at the first filtering level, is saved in the cache file and lifts on its own when it expires. A pinned node ignores blocks.

## Idle power saving

When the group has had no new connection for `idle_timeout`, health checks and `answer` rechecks pause; they resume with the next connection. While the device or network is paused (a sleeping phone, for example), every background task is skipped.

## Network changes

When sing-box detects a network change, Smart treats every member as available again, clears the consecutive check failures and the failure-flood state, and, while the group is in use, runs a check right away. Scores, blocks and learned quality are kept.
